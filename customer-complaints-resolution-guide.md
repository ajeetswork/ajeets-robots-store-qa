## Resolution workflow

1. Slack complaint arrives in #customer-complaint
2. Map to Printify product ID
3. Log in Notion Complaints database (`398c1db3-c3be-810e-ab15-d63297d613f5`)
4. Assign owner, set priority
5. Fix in Printify / Wix
6. Close loop in Slack + Notion

## Prevention

- Run weekly catalog QA against complaint patterns
- Expand thin descriptions proactively (see complaint #4)
- Audit title quality for all 28 Printify products
